Lingxiang Jia

（贾凌翔）

Assistant Professor, Ph.D Advisor
Zhejiang University

I am an Assistant Professor in the College of Computer Science and Technology and the State Key Laboratory of Blockchain and Data Security at Zhejiang University. Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Zhejiang University and my B.S. in Software Engineering at Nankai University.

My research lies at the intersection of AI for science and computer vision, with a particular focus on developing a unified multi-agent paradigm for cross-disciplinary scientific research.

  • AI applications for cross-disciplinary scientific research
  • Multi-omics integration and medical image analysis
  • Representation learning for biomedical data

For prospective students: If you are interested in working with me, please feel free to contact me via e-mail.
